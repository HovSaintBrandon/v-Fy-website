How It Works

From Booking to PDF Report in 4 Steps

Simple enough for everyone, powerful enough for every class.

01

Class Rep Books Venue

The class representative selects an available venue and books it for the upcoming lecture or session.

02

Start Attendance Session

Once class begins, the rep starts attendance. A QR code or Nearby Share session is generated instantly.

03

Students Scan & Confirm

Students scan the QR code, tap NFC, or connect via Nearby Share. One tap and they're marked present.

04

Session Closes, PDF Saved

When class ends, the session closes automatically. A PDF attendance report is generated and saved.
