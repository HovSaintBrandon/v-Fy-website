Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 23, 2025

1. Who We Are

VenueFY is a personal, unofficial project developed by an independent JKUAT student to improve campus life. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by JKUAT or any official university body. This policy explains how we handle your data in compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

2. Data We Collect

We collect minimal data:

  • Name and Registration Number (for attendance & lecture viewing)
  • Attendance records
  • Optional testimonials

We do NOT collect emails, phones, locations, or sensitive data.

3. How We Use Your Data

Data is used only for:

  • Enabling attendance marking
  • Venue booking verification
  • Generating class attendance reports
  • Displaying approved testimonials

All processing follows DPA principles.

4. Storage & Security

Data is encrypted and stored securely. Retention: Attendance deleted after 60 days post-session. No selling or sharing with third parties and if so the only third party is me.

5. Your Rights

Under Kenya DPA, you can:

  • Access, rectify, or delete your data
  • Withdraw consent
  • Complain to ODPC

Contact us to exercise rights.
